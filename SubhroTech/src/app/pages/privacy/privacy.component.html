Privacy Policy

How we protect client information, project data, and communications.

1. Data Collection & Purpose

We collect only the technical and corporate data strictly required to formulate proposals, provide technical consulting, execute software development projects, and invoice for services rendered.

2. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

All source code, database architectures, client API keys, and corporate communications are protected under strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). We never sell, monetize, or publicly disclose client proprietary logic or confidential data.

3. Technical Inquiries & Newsletter

Contact inquiries submitted through our website are securely routed to our engineering management team. Newsletter subscribers receive only periodic engineering and architectural dispatches, with single-click unsubscription supported at all times.
