Privacy Policy

Your privacy is critically important to us. At Subhro Tech, we have a few fundamental principles regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We only ask for personal information when we truly need it to provide a service to you. We collect it by fair and lawful means, with your knowledge and consent. We also let you know why we’re collecting it and how it will be used.

  • Personal Data: Name, Email, Phone Number, Educational details for internship processing.
  • Usage Data: Information on how you access and use our website.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Process your internship application and issue certificates.
  • Communicate with you regarding your internship progress.
  • Send you updates about new opportunities (only if you subscribe).
  • Improve our services and website functionality.

3. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

4. Cookies

Our website uses "cookies" to collect information and improve our Service. You have the option to either accept or refuse these cookies, and know when a cookie is being sent to your computer. If you choose to refuse our cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our Service.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, we advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
