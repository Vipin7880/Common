Your privacy is critically important to us. At Subhro Tech, we handle sensitive corporate data with the highest security standards.

We only ask for information when we truly need it to provide our IT consulting and software development services to you. We collect it by fair and lawful means, with your knowledge and consent.

We value your trust. We adhere strictly to Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) signed with our clients. Your proprietary source code, trade secrets, and business logic shared during development are stored in secure, access-controlled repositories.

We may employ third-party companies (such as hosting providers, payment gateways, or AI model providers) to facilitate our services. These third parties have access to necessary data only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We advise you to review this page for any changes. We will notify active clients of material changes directly via email.
