Terms of Service

Legal terms governing client engagements and software development deliverables.

1. Engagement Framework

Subhro Tech provides custom software architecture, web application development, cloud deployment, and IT consulting services. All client engagements are governed by an executed Statement of Work (SOW) or formal technical services agreement that details project milestones, payment terms, and deliverables.

2. Project Timelines & Collaboration

Estimated delivery dates rely upon timely client reviews, necessary third-party API credentials, and prompt milestone sign-offs. Scope alterations requested following project commencement are evaluated and documented in revised sprint statements.

3. Intellectual Property Rights

Upon final settlement of project invoices according to the SOW schedule, full ownership and intellectual property rights for custom code, database structures, and digital assets transfer to the client. Pre-existing open-source modules and proprietary utility libraries remain licensed under their respective terms.

4. Confidentiality & Security

Subhro Tech operates under mutual non-disclosure obligations. Proprietary business logic, source code, data schemas, and API secrets shared during development are strictly isolated in access-controlled environments.
