Cancellation and Refund Policy

1. Registration Fees

Fees paid for internship registration are generally non-refundable. This fees covers administrative costs, access to our learning portal, and setup of your internship workspace.

2. Cancellation Policy

If you wish to cancel your enrollment, you must do so within 24 hours of registration. Cancellations made within this window may be eligible for a refund, subject to a 10% processing fee.

3. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, it will be processed within 5-7 business days. The amount will be credited back to the original method of payment.

4. Exceptions

Refunds may be considered in exceptional circumstances (e.g., medical emergencies) at the sole discretion of Subhro Tech management. Supporting documentation will be required.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our Cancellation and Refund Policy, please contact us at: support@subhrotech.com
