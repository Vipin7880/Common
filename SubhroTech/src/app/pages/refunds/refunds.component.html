Billing & Refund Policy

Commercial framework and payment milestone policies for software development services.

1. Milestone-Based Commercial Model

Subhro Tech projects are billed against pre-agreed milestone schedules defined in the Statement of Work (SOW). Each milestone represents tangible deliverables (e.g. system architecture approval, staging feature release, production cutover).

2. Sprint & Retainer Engagements

Retainer-based and dedicated engineering team services are billed at the beginning of each sprint cycle. Clients may pause or terminate retainer agreements with 14 business days written notice.

3. Disputed Billing & Inquiries

For questions regarding invoices, milestone calculations, or contract reconciliations, direct your inquiry to our billing operations team at contact@subhrotech.com.
