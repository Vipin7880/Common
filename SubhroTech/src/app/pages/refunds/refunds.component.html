Cancellation & Refund Policy

Our policies regarding project cancellations, deposits, and refunds.

1. Project Deposits

To initiate a software development project, Subhro Tech typically requires an upfront deposit. This deposit secures your place in our development queue and covers initial discovery and architectural planning. Initial deposits are non-refundable once project discovery has commenced.

2. Cancellation Policy

If you wish to cancel an ongoing project, you must provide written notice. You will be invoiced for all work completed up to the date of cancellation based on our hourly rates or project milestones. Any pre-paid amounts exceeding the value of work completed will be eligible for a refund.

3. Hosting and Maintenance

Payments for ongoing hosting, AI chatbot usage limits, and monthly maintenance retainers are generally non-refundable. Cancellations must be made 30 days prior to the next billing cycle to avoid being charged.

4. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved by our billing department, it will be processed within 7-14 business days. The amount will be credited back to the original method of payment or via bank transfer.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or disputes regarding invoices or refunds, please contact our billing team at: contact@subhrotech.com
